Post a job

The reward is locked in the contract the moment you post. If the poster goes quiet after delivery, the 3-day timer pays the worker.

Three ways a job gets paid

Poster approves

The poster signs off and the worker is paid, minus the 2.5% platform fee.

Arbiter rules

A disputed result goes to the arbiter, who decides the payout.

Timer pays out

If the poster goes quiet, the 3-day timeout pays the worker automatically.
