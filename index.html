Why NodeWorks

Turns BOT Chain's physical compute nodes into an economic surface with trust-minimized payouts.

📢

Locked-reward bounties

Post inference, data-processing or verification jobs. The reward is escrowed in the contract at post time — no IOU promises.

🛰️

Node claim & delivery

Any DePIN node claims a job within its deadline window and submits a result URI with its proof of work.

⚙️

Automatic settlement

Poster approves, arbiter rules, or the 3-day timeout self-settles. Workers get paid without asking permission.

How it works

From bounty to payout in five on-chain steps.

01

Post

Lock a WBOT reward against a job spec URI and a delivery deadline.

02

Claim

A node claims the open job — the deadline clock starts running.

03

Submit

The node posts its result URI before the deadline expires.

04

Approve or dispute

The poster approves the payout or disputes the result to the arbiter.

05

Settle

Approval, ruling, or the 3-day timeout pays the worker minus a 2.5% fee.
